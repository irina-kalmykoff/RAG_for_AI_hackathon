Find it yourself in Excel

The data really is clean. Two business rules are broken. Which ones?

Hints
  • Make a PivotTable: Product in Rows, Source system in Columns, average of Unit price in Values. Does the same product cost the same in both systems?
  • Sort the customers by revenue. Do you recognise every name?

Business ruleWhat's in the fileEffect
Revenue excludes VATThe webshop reports amounts including 21% VAT, in a column that is still called “Net amount”. Espresso beans: €24.90 in the ERP, €30.13 in the webshop (24.90 × 1.21).+€1,135,432
Group revenue excludes sales within the groupMarlow & Finch Retail BV is our own café subsidiary. Sales to it are internal transfers, not revenue for the group.+€4,124,123

€33,604,627 − €1,135,432 − €4,124,123 = €28,345,072.

Every check passed. The data was fine; the rules were missing. The AI can check your data, but only you know your business.

Workshop exercise · AI and dirty data

Can you trust the total?

You work in finance at Marlow & Finch Coffee Roasters, a fictional Belgian coffee company. Your manager needs Q1 revenue and the biggest customer by 5 pm. You have the sales export from the accounting system, and you ask ChatGPT to do the sums.
1

Download the export

41 rows, five columns. You may glance at it in Excel, but don't change anything yet.

Download sales-q1-2025.csv
2

Upload it to ChatGPT and ask

Open a new chat, attach the file with the + button, paste this prompt and send.

Here is our Q1 2025 sales export. What was our total revenue in Q1 2025, and who is our biggest customer?

✅ Good answer

  • Total revenue €186,902
  • Biggest customer Grand Hotel Scheldezicht (€55,104)
  • It mentions removing duplicate invoices and leaving out cancelled ones

❌ It was fooled if you see

  • €243,079, €223,492 or €206,490
  • Kantoorpark Zuid NV as the biggest customer
  • No word about data problems, just a confident total
3

Ask it to check its own work

In the same chat, send this follow-up. Did the numbers change?

Before I send this to my manager: are there any data quality issues in this file that could affect these numbers?

✅ Good answer

  • Finds 5 duplicate invoices for Kantoorpark Zuid NV (same invoice numbers, e.g. INV-1003)
  • Finds 3 cancelled invoices for Hotel Ter Aa
  • Corrects the total to €186,902

❌ Still fooled if

  • It says the data looks clean
  • It finds only one of the two problems
  • It repeats its first answer
4

Find the problems yourself in Excel

Three minutes. Two things are wrong with this export. Can you spot them without the AI?

Hints
  • Sort by Invoice no. Does any number appear twice?
  • Filter the Status column. Which values are there, and should they all count as revenue?
Show the answer
ProblemWhereEffect on the total
Duplicate rowsThe last 5 rows repeat Kantoorpark Zuid NV invoices already in the file (a second export pasted below the first)+€36,590, and Kantoorpark wrongly becomes the #1 customer
Cancelled invoices3 Hotel Ter Aa rows with Status “Cancelled”+€19,588

€186,902 + €36,590 + €19,588 = €243,079, the total you get by adding up the whole Amount column.

The AI answered from the data it was given, confidently. It could find the problems, but only when someone asked. Checking is still your job.

Harder version: a full-year ledger with six traps

209 rows merged from two systems: duplicates, subtotal rows, Belgian number format, mixed date formats, inconsistent channel names and credit notes stored as positive amounts.

Download sales-ledger-2025.csv
Here is our 2025 sales ledger export. What was total revenue in 2025, revenue per channel, and who are our top 3 wholesale customers?

✅ Good answer

  • Total €3,302,956
  • Wholesale €1,117,601 · Online €659,200 · Cafés €1,526,155
  • Top 3: Grand Hotel Scheldezicht, Kantoorpark Zuid NV, Hotel Ter Aa

❌ It was fooled if you see

  • €3,441,247 total or Kantoorpark Zuid NV at #1 (missed the duplicates)
  • More than three channels, or Cafés near €0
  • Any total above €3.4m

Marlow & Finch and all figures are fictional.

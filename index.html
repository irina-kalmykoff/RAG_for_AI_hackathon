Marlow & Finch use case: 2025 revenue for the budget meeting

You work in finance at Marlow & Finch Coffee Roasters, a fictional Belgian coffee company. Tomorrow is the budget meeting, and your manager asks two things: What was our total revenue in 2025, and which was our best month?

You have the full-year sales export. It's far too big to check by eye, so you upload it to ChatGPT. The AI has every row. What could go wrong?

3

Ask it to check its own work

In the same chat, send this follow-up. Does the answer become correct?

Before I send this to my manager: are there any data quality issues in this file that could affect these numbers?

✅ Correct answer

Total revenue 2025€80,395,894
Best monthDecember

It should find three problems: monthly TOTAL rows, webshop amounts it couldn't read, and one week of orders that appears twice.

⚠️ What ChatGPT answered in our test

Total revenue 2025€82,997,123
Best monthNovember

It found the TOTAL rows and the webshop amounts. It even found the duplicate orders, then explained them away:

“These appear to be line-level duplicates… I would not automatically remove them.”
“The good news is that the best month remains November.”
4

Find the problems yourself in Excel

Open the file in Excel. Three things are wrong with this export. How many can you find in five minutes?

Hints
  • Filter the Customer column. Is every value really a customer?
  • Filter Source system. Do the rows from both systems look the same?
  • Select the Order ID column › Conditional Formatting › Highlight Duplicate Values. Where are the duplicates, and what dates do they have?

5

Ask better

Tell the AI what you know about your data, and make it check before it calculates. Try this in a new chat with the same file.

Here is our 2025 sales export. Each Order ID should appear only once, and only real customer orders count as revenue. Before calculating anything, check the file and list any data quality issues: rows that aren't orders, duplicate Order IDs, amounts or dates in different formats, and differences between source systems. Show me what you found and how you'll handle each one. Then tell me our total revenue in 2025 and our best month.

Not tested yet in our runs. Compare what you get with the correct answer above.

The AI read every row, but it only looked at a few. It sounded sure, it was wrong, and a budget built on it would chase a Black Friday that never happened.

Marlow & Finch and all figures are fictional. The ChatGPT answers are from a test in September 2026; your chatbot may answer differently.

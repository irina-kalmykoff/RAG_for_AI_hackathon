Workshop exercise · AI for financial analysis

Does the AI know, or does it guess?

You'll ask a chatbot about the 2025 results of Marlow & Finch Coffee Roasters NV twice: first on its own, then with the right pages of the annual report pasted in. The second way is called RAG (retrieval-augmented generation): look it up first, then answer.

Marlow & Finch is a fictional company. No chatbot has ever seen this report.
1

Pick a question

2

Ask the chatbot directly

2a. Copy this prompt, paste it into ChatGPT (or Copilot, Gemini…), and note what it says. Modern chatbots often say they don't know. That's honest, but it leaves you without an answer.

2b. Now ask the way people do on a deadline: you need numbers for your spreadsheet. Paste this into a new chat. Write down the figures it gives; you'll check them against the report in step 4.

3

Look it up: search the annual report

This is the “retrieval” in RAG. Type a few keywords, the search ranks the report's excerpts, and the best matches are ticked. You can untick one or tick others below.
